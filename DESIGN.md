# Design Direction: Chesta Sitorus Portfolio

## 1. Identity & Purpose
- **Owner**: Chesta Sitorus
- **Role**: S1 Teknologi Informasi Student at Telkom University & Web/Software Developer
- **Objective**: An authentic, clean, human-crafted developer portfolio with a light/white aesthetic that stands out through craftsmanship, typography, and clear visual hierarchy, completely free from generic AI slop tropes (no neon radial glows, no blueprint grids, no purple gradients, no fake percentage bars, no template buzzwords).

## 2. Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced - Confident, editorial, approachable)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Consistent structure with thoughtful compositional variety)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Refined micro-transitions and hover states only; no gratuitous floating or template bounce)

## 3. Color System (White Theme Foundation)
- **Background Main**: `#fafafa` (warm, natural paper white - avoids sterile clinical glare)
- **Background Secondary**: `#f4f5f7` (subtle contrast for section banding)
- **Card Surface**: `#ffffff` (crisp, elevated, tactile)
- **Border / Divider**: `#e5e7eb` (tactile, soft slate edge, 1px)
- **Border Hover**: `#94a3b8` (honest slate focus)
- **Text Primary (Ink)**: `#0f172a` (deep slate/ink, WCAG AAA 14:1+ contrast)
- **Text Secondary**: `#334155` (crisp readable charcoal)
- **Text Muted**: `#64748b` (subtle captions and metadata)
- **Accent Primary**: `#1e40af` (deep royal cobalt blue, timeless, professional, human)
- **Accent Light / Tint**: `#eff6ff` (soft cobalt wash for active badges)
- **Success / Available**: `#15803d` (natural emerald green for availability status)

## 4. Typography
- **Headings**: `Outfit`, sans-serif (optical sizing, tight tracking, confident weight)
- **Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (balanced line height 1.65, excellent readability)
- **Technical/Tags**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (used exclusively for tech labels and code indicators)

## 5. Architectural Decisions & Anti-Slop Rationale
- **Color Choice**: Deep cobalt `#1e40af` and deep ink `#0f172a` against crisp paper white `#fafafa` creates an authentic editorial feel rather than the ubiquitous purple/cyan AI SaaS gradient.
- **Layout**: Clear asymmetric hierarchy with strong typography and white space as structure, rather than repetitive bento boxes.
- **Skills Presentation**: Grouped capability matrix (Frontend, Backend, Tools) with real technologies and tools instead of arbitrary percentage bars ("Problem Solver 96%").
- **Spacing**: Generous, rhythmic vertical rhythm (80px - 100px section spacing) allowing content to breathe naturally.
- **Decoration**: No ambient glow blobs, no cursor trails, no grid lines. Subtle 1px borders and gentle 2px-6px box-shadows give honest physical elevation.
